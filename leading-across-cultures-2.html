Dilemma exercise

What would a good leader do?

Gabriela is a successful project manager from Brazil. She moves to Sweden to lead a new team. The team is friendly, but they question her proposals in meetings and keep giving opinions after she makes a decision. Why? And what should she do?

Solve six leadership dilemmas, get feedback on your choices, and discuss them with a partner or your class.

The idea behind the exercise

Psychologist Geert Hofstede uses the term power distance to describe how much people in a culture accept that power is not shared equally.

    Debriefing
    / 18

    Three ideas to remember

    • Culture shapes expectations. Gabriela's Brazilian team and her Swedish team both wanted to work well. They simply expected different things from a leader.
    • Neither style is wrong. A directive style worked in Brazil. A coaching style worked in Sweden. A good leader reads the situation.
    • Talk about it openly. Gabriela's team improved when she asked for their views, explained her own, and everyone adapted.
