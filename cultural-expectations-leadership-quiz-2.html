Leading across cultures

Six realistic workplace dilemmas about authority, initiative, feedback and cultural expectations. Choose the response you would use, then examine its likely impact.

6 dilemmasMeet different leadership challenges, one at a time.
4 responses eachChoose one. Responses range from strong to less effective.
Learn and discussGet feedback after every decision and finish with a debrief.

How to play

  1. Read the situation and consider what each person may expect from a manager.
  2. Choose the response you would take in that situation. There is no score for simply guessing a “perfect” answer.
  3. Read the feedback, which explains the likely benefits and risks of your choice.
  4. Complete the debriefing questions alone or with a partner.

The situations explore cultural expectations, not fixed rules about nationalities. Individuals and organisations vary, even within the same country.

1. What should a manager do when a team openly questions a decision?

When is debate useful, and when must a manager make the final decision?

2. How can a manager show authority without limiting initiative?

Think about clear goals, boundaries, trust and responsibility.

3. What can cause a cultural misunderstanding at work?

Give an example of how the same behaviour might be interpreted in two different ways.

4. Should a manager adapt their style to the team?

What should remain consistent, even when the manager changes their approach?

5. Which response did you choose differently from your partner?

Explain your reasons and identify one possible advantage or risk in each approach.
