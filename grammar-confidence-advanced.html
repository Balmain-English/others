Assessment Format & Rules

  • You will review 20 upper-intermediate sentences (40% work context and 60% daily life context).
  • Determine whether each sentence is Grammatically Correct or contains a Grammar Error.
  • Select a confidence wager for each item based on your certainty:
    • 1 Point: Low certainty or educated guess.
    • 2 Points: Moderate certainty.
    • 3 Points: High certainty.
  • Scoring Mechanism: Correct evaluations earn your wagered points (+1 to +3). Incorrect evaluations deduct your wagered points (-1 to -3).
