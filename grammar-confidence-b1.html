B1 English Grammar Self-Assessment

Evaluate your understanding of intermediate B1 English grammar in daily life and work situations.

Assessment Structure & Rules

  • You will review 20 sentences (40% work context and 60% daily life context).
  • Determine whether each sentence is Grammatically Correct or contains a Grammar Error.
  • Choose a confidence wager for each item based on your certainty:
    • 1 Point: Low certainty or educated guess.
    • 2 Points: Moderate certainty.
    • 3 Points: High certainty.
  • Scoring: Correct answers add your wagered points (+1 to +3). Incorrect answers deduct your wagered points (-1 to -3).
