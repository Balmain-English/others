How narrative tenses relate to each other on a time sequence:

1
Past Perfect had + V3
Earliest event before another past action. "I had forgot..."
2
Past Continuous was/were + -ing
Background action in progress in the past. "While I was walking..."
3
Past Simple verb-ed / V2
Main completed action or interruption. "...it started to rain."
NOW
Present Perfect have/has + V3
Life experience up to now (unspecified time). "I have visited Rome."
Present Perfect have/has + past participle

When to use:

  • General life experiences ("Have you ever...?").
  • Actions in the past where time is NOT mentioned.
  • Actions with a direct connection to the present moment.
Examples:

✓ "I have lost my keys." (I don't have them now)

✓ "Have you ever eaten sushi?" (In your lifetime)

Past Simple verb-ed / V2

When to use:

  • Completed actions at a specific finished time in the past.
  • Main chronological events in a story (First... then... next...).
  • Asking "When...?" or "Where...?" follow-up questions.
Examples:

✓ "I lost my keys yesterday afternoon."

✓ "We arrived at 8 PM and bought tickets."

Past Continuous was/were + verb-ing

When to use:

  • Background actions in progress at a specific past moment.
  • Longer actions interrupted by a shorter Past Simple event (while / as).
  • Setting the atmosphere/scene at the start of a narrative.
Examples:

✓ "While I was walking home, it started to rain."

✓ "At 10 AM, everyone was working quietly."

Past Perfect had + past participle

When to use:

  • Action that happened BEFORE another event in the past story.
  • Clarifying cause and effect or sequence in a narrative.
  • Keywords: already, never, realized that, because, before.
Examples:

✓ "When I arrived, the train had already left."

✓ "I realized that I had forgot my wallet."

Narrative Follow-up Cheat Sheet

How to transition from Present Perfect to Narrative Tenses

Step 1: Start with Present Perfect

"Have you ever [verb in V3]...?"

Partner says: "Yes, I have!"

Step 2: Switch to Past Simple for Details
  • "When did it happen?"
  • "Where were you?"
  • "What did you do next?"
Step 3: Probe Background (Past Continuous)
  • "What were you doing when it happened?"
  • "Who were you talking to at that moment?"
Step 4: Probe Earlier Events (Past Perfect)
  • "Had you planned to do that before?"
  • "What had happened right before that moment?"
