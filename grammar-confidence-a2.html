Check your understanding of basic English grammar structures in everyday and work contexts.

Assessment Structure & Rules

  • You will answer 20 sentences (40% work context and 60% daily life context).
  • Decide if each sentence is Grammatically Correct or contains an Error.
  • Choose a confidence wager for each item:
    • 1 Point: Low certainty or guess.
    • 2 Points: Medium certainty.
    • 3 Points: High certainty.
  • Scoring: Correct answers add your wagered points (+1 to +3). Incorrect answers deduct your wagered points (-1 to -3).
